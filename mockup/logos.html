Four marks, seven treatments

Play, Route, Fork and Four. Each shape is drawn once and every column is only a change of stroke weight, node size and hue, so nothing drifts between treatments — pick a cell and it is one SVG, not a redraw.

All four were redrawn to fill the icon grid instead of floating inside it; at a heavy stroke the old geometry closed its own counters. Column one is the weight the site ships today, kept only as the thing to beat. Judge on the 16 and 24 pixel row, not the tile.

Name a mark and a treatment — play heavy, four knockout — and it becomes website/logo.svg plus the glyph in scripts/make-icon.py, which regenerates the .icns, .ico and .png.
